# CampusSwap

**Pass it on, on campus.** CampusSwap is a campus-only app where students give away, swap or sell dorm items, and agree on pickup in the app. Only verified students can join.

This site documents the project end to end: the product and its research, the UX/UI design, the terms behind it, and a working Telegram bot version.
