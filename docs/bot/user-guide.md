# How students use it

## Menu and commands

| Button | Command | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🔎 Browse | `/browse` | See items from other students, your building first |
| ➕ Post an item | `/post` | List something you no longer need |
| 📦 My items | `/myitems` | Your listings and their status; remove available ones |
| 📥 My requests | `/myrequests` | Items you asked for and their status |
| 👤 Profile | `/profile` | Your rating, items given and received |
| ❓ Help | `/help` | How CampusSwap works |
| | `/building` | Change your dorm building |
| | `/cancel` | Stop posting an item |

## Giving an item away

1. Tap **➕ Post an item**.
2. Send a photo, or `/skip`.
3. Send a short title, e.g. *Desk lamp, IKEA*.
4. Choose a category and condition.
5. Choose **Free**, **Swap** or **Sell** (then send a price in rubles).
6. Tap the suggested pickup spot or type your own.
7. Send pickup times separated by commas: `Today 18:00, Today 19:30, Tomorrow 12:00`.
8. Check the preview and tap **✅ Publish**.

When someone requests it, you get their name, building, rating and chosen time with **Accept** and **Decline** buttons. Accepting one request declines the others automatically.

## Getting an item

1. Tap **🔎 Browse** and choose a category or **Free only**.
2. Flip through cards with ◀ ▶.
3. Tap **🙋 Request** and choose a pickup time.
4. Wait for the owner to accept. You'll get the pickup spot, time and a link to message them.

## The handover

1. Meet at the pickup spot at the agreed time.
2. Either of you taps **✅ Handed over**. The item is marked as given.
3. Both of you rate the exchange from 1 to 5 stars.

Can't make it? Tap **✖ Cancel pickup**: the other person is notified and the item becomes available again.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 available | Listed and visible in Browse |
| 🟡 reserved | A pickup is agreed |
| ✅ given away | Handed over |
| ⏳ waiting for the owner | Your request hasn't been answered yet |
| 🤝 pickup agreed | The owner accepted your request |
| ❌ declined | The owner declined, or gave it to someone else |
